About

She is a Machine Learning researcher at the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA), where she works on synthetic data as a privacy-enhancing technology for healthcare data sharing. Her work is embedded in the Clinical Practice Research Datalink (CPRD) ecosystem, contributing to the development and evaluation of synthetic data generation frameworks for primary care data, including the CPRD high-fidelity synthetic datasets now publicly available for AI and machine learning research.

Alongside her role at MHRA, she is pursuing a PhD in Computer Science at Brunel University London. Her doctoral research builds directly on this work, focusing on the rigorous privacy evaluation of synthetic clinical data, with a particular emphasis on membership inference attacks and their role as auditing tools for differential privacy guarantees.

Feel free to reach out at ylenia.rotalinti@gmail.com.
