About

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Brunel University London, working at the intersection of synthetic data generation, privacy, and healthcare AI. My research focuses on the evaluation of privacy-preserving synthetic data for healthcare data sharing, with a particular emphasis on membership inference attacks and differential privacy in clinical datasets.

My work is embedded in the MHRA/CPRD ecosystem, where I contribute to the development and evaluation of synthetic data frameworks for primary care data — including the CPRD high-fidelity synthetic datasets now publicly available for AI and machine learning research. I am broadly interested in trustworthy AI, data-centric evaluation methodologies, and the regulatory dimensions of AI as a medical device.

I am also co-founder of Emulate, a synthetic data platform spinning out of the CPRD/MHRA research programme, and co-organiser of the A2I (Accelerator to Impact) programme supporting early-career researchers in Health and Life Sciences commercialisation.

I am always happy to chat — feel free to reach out at ylenia.rotalinti@gmail.com.
